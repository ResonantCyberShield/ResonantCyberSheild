# VeriHuman: Stage 2 niche research (October 2026)

## Demand: strong and well evidenced

- **Losses are large and rising.** US consumers reported **$1.16B lost to romance scams in the first nine months of 2025** (FTC). Reports were up **22%** on the same period of 2024: 55,604 reports. Full-year 2025 figures cited elsewhere are about $1.48B.
  - [FTC data via Central Oregon Daily](https://www.centraloregondaily.com/news/consumer/ftc-romance-scams-1-billion-losses-2025/article_c32c7fc5-c3a9-4cdc-8f4b-c80293080267.html)
  - [Dating Industry Insights](https://www.datingindustryinsights.com/news/romance-scams-outpace-other-frauds)
  - [FTC, social media scams, April 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/04/new-ftc-data-show-people-have-lost-billions-social-media-scams)
- **Median loss:** about **$2,218** per victim (Q3 2025).
- **About 60% of 2025 romance-scam losses started on social media, not dating apps.** VeriHuman's "any stranger online" framing fits that better than a dating-only pitch.
- **Crypto is now the top payment method**, at about 34% of losses, followed by gift cards at about 17%. These two are the heaviest-weighted categories in the message engine.
- **Ages 55–64 have the highest total losses.** Adults 60+ lost about $584M.
  - [romance scam statistics roundup](https://www.arnaques-rencontres.fr/en/articles/romance-scam-statistics)
  - [marriagescience.com](https://marriagescience.com/romance-scam-statistics/)
- The sources' totals don't all agree ($1.16B for nine months vs $1.48B for the full year vs older $1.3B figures). Treat the FTC's own releases as authoritative.

## Competition: moderate, and clustered on identity lookup

- **Reverse image and identity search:** Social Catfish (the established paid leader), PeopleFinder, PimEyes and Catfish Finder.
  - [peoplefinder.app comparison](https://peoplefinder.app/blog/5-best-catfish-detection-tools-in-2026-actually-work)
  - [Catfish Finder (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/catfish-finder/id6744071884)
  - [Social Catfish alternatives](https://cheatscanx.com/blog/social-catfish-alternative/)
- **Protection built into the dating apps:** Bumble's Deception Detector, and photo verification on Hinge, Tinder and Bumble. These only protect users *inside* those apps, while most losses now start on social media. [socialcatfish.com](https://socialcatfish.com/scamfish/top-dating-apps-for-online-dating-in-2026-safety-comes-first/)
- **Generic deepfake detectors** (Reality Defender, Hive, Sightengine) sell APIs to developers, not consumer apps.

**Score: high demand, moderate competition, with a clear gap.**
- Competitors answer "is this photo someone else's?"
- Nobody in the consumer space combines all of these:
  1. scam-script analysis of the messages themselves;
  2. voice-clone detection;
  3. a running history per contact;
  4. on-device privacy.
- That combination is VeriHuman's defensible angle.

## Recommendations

1. **Lead with messages, not photos.** Social Catfish owns photo lookup. The message and thread analysis, which spots crypto and gift-card scripts, is the differentiator and matches where the money is actually lost.
2. **Reverse image search is the single most-cited verification step.** The app currently links out to Google Lens and TinEye. Consider a one-tap "Search this photo" on the photo result to keep users in the flow.
3. **The audience skews older (55+).** The bold Night Scanner look appeals to younger users. Make sure text size, contrast and plain-language copy work for older users, who also need Light mode to stay easy to find.
4. **Position as "any stranger online"**, with social media first and dating apps second, in the store listing.
5. Publisher: assumed to be **Daybreak Labs** (app publishing).
